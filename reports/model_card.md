## Limitations and risks

- Synthetic data: the fraud patterns are the ones the generator injects, so the model is evaluated on patterns it could in principle learn perfectly. Real fraud adapts.
- Labels are assumed complete after the label delay (3 days); real chargebacks trickle in for weeks and some fraud is never reported.
- Scores are not calibrated probabilities (class weighting shifts them); use the threshold shipped with the artefact, or recalibrate before reading them as rates.
- Hour-of-day uses UTC, not the cardholder's local time.
- Country and device signals can encode protected characteristics in real data and need a fairness review before any real use.
